# Contributing to Updated Morning and Evening

Thank you for your interest in **Updated Morning and Evening**!

## Reporting Typos or Textual Improvements

With 732 daily devotions across both the **Modern Edition (BSB)** and **Classic Edition (KJV)**, careful proofreading is always welcome. If you spot a typo, formatting glitch, or a sentence in the Modern Edition that could be clearer or more faithful to Spurgeon's original phrasing:

1. Open an issue on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Aussie-App-Studio/Updated-Morning-and-Evening/issues).
2. Include the **Month and Day** (e.g., *March 14 · Evening*), the **Edition** (*Modern* or *Classic*), and the phrase in question.

## Reporting Bugs or Suggesting Features

- Please include your Android version and device model when reporting UI or layout bugs.
- Feature suggestions that align with the app's calm, distraction-free, 100% offline philosophy are warmly welcomed.

## Pull Requests

1. Fork the repository and create a feature branch.
2. Verify that the project compiles cleanly with `./gradlew compileDebugKotlin`.
3. Ensure that no external tracking, analytics, or network dependencies are introduced.
