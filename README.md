# Updated Morning and Evening

**Charles H. Spurgeon’s beloved daily devotional — thoughtfully renewed in clear, natural contemporary English alongside the original Victorian classic.**

Built for Android with Kotlin & Jetpack Compose. **100% offline. Zero internet permissions. No ads, no accounts, and no tracking.**

---

## Why Updated Morning and Evening?

For over 150 years, Charles Haddon Spurgeon’s *Morning and Evening* has guided millions of Christians through the Scriptures—offering 732 rich, Christ-centered meditations (one for every morning and every evening of the year, including Leap Day).

Yet for many modern readers, Spurgeon’s 1860s Victorian prose, archaic Elizabethan idioms, and unbroken 300-word paragraphs can create an unintended barrier. **Updated Morning and Evening** removes that barrier without losing a single drop of Spurgeon’s theological depth, pastoral warmth, or literary beauty:

- **Modern Edition (BSB):** Carefully updates obsolete vocabulary and archaic phrasing into clear, natural contemporary English while faithfully preserving 100% of Spurgeon’s meaning, imagery, and conviction—paired with the public-domain **Berean Standard Bible (BSB)**.
- **Classic Edition (KJV):** Preserves Spurgeon’s original 19th-century text word-for-word, paired with the **King James Version (KJV)**.
- **One-Tap Switching:** Toggle effortlessly between *Modern* and *Classic* at any time while reading.

## Key Features

### Six Thoughtful Reading Layouts
Choose the presentation that best fits how you read and reflect:
1. **Classic Book:** Fine-press book typography with centered chapter headings, framed epigraph, drop-cap initial, and literary paragraphs.
2. **Standard Devotional:** Intuitive modern layout with Morning/Evening header, accent-ruled verse callout, and breathable 2–3 sentence paragraphs.
3. **Sentence Cadence:** Tames long Victorian sentences by starting each sentence on a fresh line and gently stepping multi-clause sentences.
4. **Guided Editorial:** Calendar masthead, quoted Scripture frame, and numbered sections with bold lead-in sentences.
5. **Reflection Cards:** Calm pill header, highlighted Scripture card, and bite-sized reflection cards.
6. **Spurgeon’s Paragraphs:** Preserves Spurgeon’s original unbroken full-length paragraph formatting.

### Crafted for Daily Devotion
- **Adaptive Reading Themes:** *Light*, *Sepia*, *Dark*, and *AMOLED Black* palettes—configurable as a single theme, automatic *Morning / Evening* themes, or *Auto (Phone)* system mode, plus a warm in-app brightness dimmer.
- **Fine Typography Controls:** Choose from *Literata Serif*, *EB Garamond*, *Sans*, *Lexend*, or *OpenDyslexic*, with customizable text size, line spacing, margins,alignment, and page-turn animations (*Slide*, *Book Page Curl*, *Depth Stack*, or *Instant*).
- **Personal Reflection Notes & Saved Readings:** Write personal notes on any devotion, bookmark favorite readings, and track your reading completion across all 732 meditations.
- **Flexible Reading Schedules:**
  - *Follow the calendar:* Opens automatically to today’s date and time of day.
  - *Start from a day I choose:* Begin at any date (such as January 1) on any day of the year and advance one day at a time.
  - *Resume where I left off:* Always reopens to the last devotion you viewed.
- **Today’s Verse Home Screen Widget:** Displays the current Morning or Evening Scripture verse and reference right on your Home screen, matching your chosen edition and theme.
- **Gentle Daily Reminders:** Optional local notifications for your Morning and Evening reading times, including the day’s Scripture verse.
- **Tap-to-Open Scripture References:** Tap any verse reference to open the full chapter in *YouVersion*, *Bible Gateway*, or *Blue Letter Bible*.
- **Full EPUB Book Export & Local JSON Backup:** Export the complete *Modern* or *Classic* edition as a standalone `.epub` e-book, and back up or restore your notes, bookmarks, and progress as a local `.json` file.

## Building from Source

1. Clone the repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Aussie-App-Studio/Updated-Morning-and-Evening.git
   cd Updated-Morning-and-Evening
   ```
2. Build or install the debug APK via Gradle:
   ```bash
   ./gradlew assembleDebug
   # or install directly to a connected Android device:
   ./gradlew installDebug
   ```
